# TradePro-v3.1
TradePro Trading Journal

TradePro Trading Journal is a professional trading journal designed to help traders track, analyze, and improve their trading performance. Every trade you take becomes valuable data, allowing you to identify strengths, eliminate mistakes, and build a consistent trading strategy.

How to Use

1. Record every trade immediately after execution.
2. Enter the trading pair or asset, entry price, stop loss, take profit, risk percentage, and trade outcome.
3. Add screenshots of your chart before and after the trade whenever possible.
4. Write down the reason for entering the trade and whether it followed your trading plan.
5. Record your emotions before, during, and after the trade.
6. Review your journal weekly and monthly to identify patterns, improve discipline, and refine your strategy.

Remember: The goal is not just to record trades—it is to become a more disciplined, data-driven, and consistently profitable trader.
